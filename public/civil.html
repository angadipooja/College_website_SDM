About

The department of Civil Engineering was started in 2008.The department has carved its name in the areas of academics, consultancy, research and community service. 7 faculty members are pursuing research in advanced areas of civil engineering. The department is actively involved in conducting workshops, site/field visits, survey camps, projects for sharing and spreading latest developments in the field of civil engineering. Specialized labs and programs like Advanced Surveying, Construction Material and Concrete Testing provide students with appropriate practical exposure.
